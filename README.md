#  Supermarket Sales Dashboard

##  Project Overview

The **Supermarket Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and monitor supermarket sales performance. It provides insights into **sales, profit, product performance, sales trends, categories, and payment methods** for the years **2021 and 2022**.

The dashboard enables users to interact with the data using filters such as **Year, Month, Sale Type, and Payment Mode**, helping identify trends and high-performing products and categories.

##  Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Track monthly and daily sales trends.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Understand customer payment preferences.
* Compare sales performance across different periods.
* Provide an interactive view of key business performance indicators.

##  Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales & Profit Analysis

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Percentage
* Monthly Sales and Profit comparison

### 2. Sales Trend Analysis

* Daily sales trend using a line chart
* Monthly sales and profit using a bar chart
* Year and month-based filtering

### 3. Product Performance

* Sales performance by product
* Identification of top-performing products
* Top product based on units sold and sales value

### 4. Category Analysis

* Sales distribution across product categories
* Category performance using a treemap
* Identification of the highest-performing category

### 5. Sales Type Analysis

* Distribution of different sales types
* Interactive filtering based on sale type

### 6. Payment Mode Analysis

* Analysis of payment preferences
* Comparison of payment modes such as **Online** and **Cash**
